Help & FAQ
How are coins calculated?
Every 1,000 verified walking steps = 1 coin (working default — final rate pending). Only server-verified steps earn coins, with a daily cap (25 walking coins in this demo) so the program stays fair and sustainable. Challenge bonuses are separate.
What does "verifying" mean?
New steps are checked against plausibility rules (speed, sensor consistency, duplicate sources) before they become spendable coins. This usually takes under an hour and protects the value of every coin in the catalogue.
Are coins money?
No. Coins are loyalty points in this program. They have no cash value, can't be sold or transferred, and can only buy reward items from the in-app catalogue under the published rules.
Why was my redemption rejected?
High-value redemptions get a human review. Common reasons are duplicate-device signals or account anomalies. If a redemption is rejected, your coins are always returned automatically, and you can appeal within 14 days.
