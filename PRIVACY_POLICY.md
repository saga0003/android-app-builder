# Privacy Policy for Calculator

**Effective date:** September 23, 2026

Calculator is a simple offline calculator application.

## Information we collect

The app does not require account registration and does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, phone calls, SMS, advertising identifiers, or other sensitive device information.

## Internet access

The app does not require an internet connection for its calculator functions.

## Advertising and analytics

The app does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because it does not collect personal information from users.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's functionality changes in a way that affects data collection or privacy, this policy will be updated accordingly.

## Contact

For privacy questions relating to this app, contact the developer through the contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing.
